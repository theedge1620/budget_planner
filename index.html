Household Budget Planner

Enter income, costs and savings on the Budget tab — every line converts to a monthly figure and rolls up to the year. Drag a row by its handle (or focus the handle and use the arrow keys) to reorder it. Untick a row's Active box to leave it out without deleting it, or tick "Skip if part-time" on costs that go away when someone works part-time. Try what-ifs on the Scenarios tab without touching your budget, and see one person at full time, 50% and not working side by side on the Overview tab. Everything is kept in this browser; export a JSON or Excel copy anytime and import it back later. Use Print report to print or save a PDF of the budget and scenario sections you choose.
